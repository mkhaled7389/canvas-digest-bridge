CANVAS DIGEST BRIDGE

Settings

Your data, your control

When enabled, the bridge reads your signed-in ASU Canvas profile, active courses, deadlines and submission status. It saves these locally in this Chrome profile. It never submits work or receives your password. Checks run about every five minutes while Chrome is running; alerts are not guaranteed to be immediate.

Read the privacy policy

OPTIONAL MOBILE ALERTS

Pushover

Configured alerts send a short sign-out message, your Pushover token and user key to Pushover. No coursework or profile information is sent. Keys are stored locally, not encrypted by this extension. Pushover is a separate service.
